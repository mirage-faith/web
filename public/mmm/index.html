Why we gather

Walking the same road

Once a month, clear your calendar and join us. We have a passion for seeing marriages thrive, and we believe a strong marriage doesn't happen by accident: it takes investment. This group exists to help couples grow, get better, and sharpen one another. It's a place to connect with others walking the same road, and a reminder that you're not alone.

Our aim: to build a firm foundation of trust, communication, and intimacy before life's inevitable storms hit, and to equip you with tools you didn't know you needed for every season.

Built together

Marriage doesn't take a semester off

MMM is unlike our other groups in one big way: we meet year-round. We're starting simple, and as we build momentum, we'll branch out together. Imagine life done in community:
